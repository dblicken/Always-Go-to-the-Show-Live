How this page works

A live-music scout for the Seacoast, Portland and Boston, with Brattleboro and Keene for trips west, updated twice a week. Each act is listed once, at its best stop on this route; other New England dates on the same tour show under Also.

Seacoast→SPACE→Portland→Deep Cuts→Boston

Act names link to the band's Bandcamp page where one exists, otherwise its own site or label page. A dotted underline means a Bandcamp search. Venues announce two to four months ahead, so later months are thin and fill in with each run; the furthest show listed now is Aug 1, 2027. Each scheduled run will add new dates as they appear.
