How this page works

A personal live-music scout for the Seacoast, Portland and Boston, updated twice a week. Each act is listed once, at its best stop on this route; other New England dates on the same tour show under Also.

Act names link to the band's Bandcamp page where one exists, otherwise its own site or label page. A dotted underline means a Bandcamp search. Announcements thin out after spring 2027; most acts haven't booked fall tours yet. Each scheduled run will add new dates as they appear.

Verified 2026-11-17 (run 13): 68 of 74 Portland and Boston picks checked against the artist's own tour listing (no same-night listing in another city); 21 also confirmed on the venue's site. Seacoast picks come straight from venue calendars.

Sources: Seacoast venue calendars (Press Room, 3S, Music Hall, Dance Hall, Word Barn, Vinegar Hill, Jonathan's, SPACE), Oxbow and the State Theatre group's calendar, and JamBase for Jimmy's, the Music Hall and the Word Barn; Songkick's Portland and Boston listings (about 920 shows). Acts were matched across regions in code by name, within 60 days of each other.

Gaps: The Casino Ballroom's 2027 season and the Music Hall's full 2027 calendar weren't posted in a readable form. Songkick misses some small-room and DIY shows. Picks are my judgment.
